What this measures, stated plainly. Compliance posture reads on-ledger capability — whether an issuer retained the power to freeze, claw back, or gate holders. It is not a compliance certification, a licence check, or legal advice. An issuer scoring well can still be in breach; one scoring poorly may be fully licensed with off-chain controls.

Reserves are invisible here. Issued supply is what the ledger says was issued. Whether it is backed is an off-chain fact no on-chain tool can verify.

Concentration figures are lower bounds when coverage is partial. Large issuers have more trustlines than one request can page through. We divide by authoritative issued supply, never by the sampled sum, and label the coverage on every response.
